Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Who we are

Usine Clips ("the App") is a personal tool operated by an individual (the "Owner") to publish videos to the Owner's own TikTok accounts. Contact: eliotgarciagaillard@gmail.com.

2. Data we access

When a TikTok account authorizes the App, the App receives, through the official TikTok APIs:

  • basic profile information (user.info.basic): open ID, display name, username and avatar;
  • an access token and a refresh token, which allow it to publish videos (video.publish);
  • the creator settings that TikTok returns for publishing, such as the available privacy levels.

The App does not access private messages, followers, contacts, analytics or any other data.

3. How we use the data

The data is used only to publish videos, together with their caption and hashtags, to the authorized account, and to check that the correct account is connected. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the Owner's personal computer, in files that only the Owner can read. No data is stored on a remote server or in a third-party database. The client secret is never exposed publicly.

5. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data goes only to TikTok, as needed to publish the videos.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the authorization is revoked. To revoke access, open TikTok > Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services (or "Manage app permissions") and remove "Usine Clips". The Owner then deletes the local token files. A deletion request can also be sent to eliotgarciagaillard@gmail.com.

7. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes

Any update to this policy will be published on this page, with a new "last updated" date.

Politique de confidentialité (résumé en français)

Usine Clips est un outil personnel qui publie des vidéos sur les propres comptes TikTok de son propriétaire. Il reçoit uniquement les informations de profil de base et les jetons d'accès nécessaires à la publication.
